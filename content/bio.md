I am an Artificial Intelligence student in the integrated B.S./M.S. program at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications. My research focuses on speech synthesis, voice conversion, and audio language models, with recent work on data-efficient speech generation for low-resource languages, EEG-based voice conversion, singing voice representation, and spoken language model alignment.

I have worked as a speech algorithm intern at Li Auto, Beijing Logic Intelligence Technology, and Shanghai Yiteou Culture Media, contributing to Instruct TTS data construction, LLM-based TTS, low-resource Thai TTS, audio understanding models, and deployment-oriented TTS/VC systems.

My recent papers have been accepted to ACL 2025 Industry Track, INTERSPEECH 2025, ICME 2026, and ICML 2026.
